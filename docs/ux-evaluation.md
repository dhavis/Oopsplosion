# UX evaluation — Oopsplosion

## Feel verdict

The fantasy is feelable on a phone. Not because "MAKE IT WORSE" is a good line. Because the loop is one look, one commit, then a cartoon the player did not have to puppeteer.

A calm portrait scene can sell annoyance in two seconds: a jammed printer, a screaming scanner, a toaster already smoking. A fat-finger fuse can be a coffee cup on a lip, a bag of frozen peas, a dripping tap. Then the player’s job is to watch. That is a phone game. Rage-room smash is also a phone game, and a worse one. Smash asks the thumb to work during the joke. Oopsplosion asks the thumb to start the joke and get out of the way.

It dies on a phone if any of these land:

- The first screen is a tool tray. Hammer, wrecking ball, giant fist, magnet, tornado — that is smash DLC, not a fuse.
- The chain is unreadable. Small portrait plus physics spray plus a HUD and the punchline never arrives. The player sees mess, not cause.
- The thumb is still invited during the chain. Extra taps, combo badges, a score popping on the best collision. The fantasy is spectatorship after authorship. Fighting the UI during the chain kills it.
- Retry is a menu. If "one more try" takes more than a beat, mastery becomes grinding.

Checkout is the portrait risk of the three MVP situations. A supermarket lane wants width. Stage it as a vertical lane: scanner and bagging area in the lower/mid third, soda pyramid and ASSISTANCE light above. Do not "fix" unreadability with pinch-zoom. Zoom is a fight. Traffic jam is out of the product.

Daily revenge is a habit product. It is not the vertical slice. Leave it out.

Verdict in one line: ship a calm office, one rubber band, one obvious cup, a chain the camera can follow, a mean end card, and an AGAIN button the thumb already knows. If that is not funny on a device in a hand, the pitch will not save it.

## First 60 seconds

No home. No shop. No "choose your destroyer." Boot is a wordmark and a cut to a situation that is already annoying.

Beat-by-beat, first session, office:

| Time | What they see | What they feel | What the thumb does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.5s | Black. Wordmark **OOPS-PLOSION**. One dry line: **MAKE IT WORSE.** | This is not a wellness app. | Nothing. No skip needed if this is short. |
| 1.5–3s | Hard cut into Sunday office, fully calm, fully lit. Title over the scene, then gone: **SUNDAY AT THE OFFICE** / **The printer has opinions.** | I know this room. I hate this room. | Nothing. Title is not a tap-through wall. |
| 3–5s | Constraint, one line, bottom-safe, huge tap to dismiss is not required — it sits, then recedes: **One rubber band. That's the whole budget.** | A rule, not a tutorial. | Optional tap anywhere to clear it faster. |
| 5–20s | Calm inspect. Printer blinking. Coffee on the lip of a desk. Phone ringing in the world, not as a UI badge. No HUD novel. Fuse objects sit in the scene like objects, not like buttons. | I am looking for the stupid thing that starts it. | Rest. Optional tiny pan only if the scene is taller than the screen. No pinch. |
| 20–28s | Player spots the cup. Stretch the rubber band (or flick the cup — constraint decides). Commit. | That was my idea. | One gesture. Fat target. Then hands off. |
| 28–48s | Chrome gone. Camera follows the chain. Slow-mo on the printer eating the cup, the paper storm, the lamp, the chair, the copier. | I am watching a joke I caused. | Nothing. A second tap does not "hit harder." |
| 48–60s | Freeze. Punchline card. **THAT ESCALATED QUICKLY.** CHAOS / SATISFACTION / THINGS THAT DEFINITELY WEREN'T YOUR FAULT. **AGAIN** and **CLIP** in the thumb zone. | Mean laugh. I can beat this spiral. | AGAIN is the only required tap. Instant reload of the same calm office. |

A first-time player must get a chain on attempt one. The office cup is the obvious fuse on purpose. Clever fuses are for retry. If attempt one is a dry fire, the genre becomes "physics homework."

Kitchen and checkout wait behind a one-line situation rail after AGAIN, or after the player has seen one punchline. Not before. The first minute proves one situation, not a campaign.

## Interaction model

The product is not "use a tool on a room." The product is "pick the fuse."

**First input is a choice.** The player is choosing which annoyance becomes the cause, under a one-line constraint. That is the game. After commit, they do not steer.

**Constraint is the toy, not the inventory.** Concept lists hammers, lasers, God fingers. A tray of those on a phone is smash. For the slice:

- Office: one rubber band, already "held." Stretch toward a target, release. The band is the constraint and the tool.
- Checkout: hands off the scanner. The legal fuse is environmental (frozen peas, coupon, soda bottle, cart). Thumb commits that, not a wrecking ball from a dock. Tapping the scanner beeps and does nothing.
- Kitchen: one object. Pick it up (hold), place or throw (release). Everything else is illegal and does not highlight as a fuse.

If a later build wants a giant fist, it is a *situation* ("God had a fist that day"), not a loadout.

**Gesture vocabulary (portrait, one thumb):**

| Gesture | Meaning | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rest / look | Inspect | Calm only |
| Tap | Commit a simple fuse (peas, dripping tap, blinking error) | Calm, if the constraint is a trigger |
| Hold + stretch + release | Rubber band | Office MVP |
| Hold + toss | Throw the one allowed object | Kitchen MVP |
| Short vertical drag | Peek the rest of a tall scene | Calm only, optional, damped |
| Second tap during chain | Ignore | Chain. Do not "charge" destruction. |

No multi-touch required. No pinch. No on-screen stick. Left and right thumbs both work because primary actions live in the lower two-thirds, and the fuse is in the scene, not in a corner exclusive to right-handers.

**Illegal moves fail as jokes, not as error toasts.** Stretch the band at the floor: it snaps, a tiny dry line (**That helped.**), calm restores in place. Do not open a fail modal. Do not deduct coins. There are no coins.

**Timer constraints** (if used) are a one-line clock, calm-only, huge type, then gone when the chain starts. A 30-second sandbox timer turns this back into smash. Prefer "one object" and "one band" over clocks in the slice.

## Readability of chaos

If they cannot parse the chain, the joke dies. Physics fidelity is not the same as readability.

**Calm is the legend.** Ordered desks, one blinking printer, one cup in silhouette, one ringing phone. High contrast objects, readable at arm's length. If the office is cluttered with twelve equal props, there is no fuse. Clutter is earned *after* the commit.

**One frame, or a tall peek.** The playable situation should read in a single portrait frame plus a little vertical overflow. The chain should mostly travel *through* that frame (down the office, down the kitchen, up the checkout lane). Camera follows the active collision with lag, not with a video-game snap.

**Causality, not particle weather.** Each link needs a readable contact: cup → printer tray, paper → fan, lamp → chair, chair → copier. Slow-mo exists to put a beat on the *link*, not on debris. If two crashes happen off-screen, the camera missed the joke.

**Hide chrome during the chain.** No constraint reminder, no pause, no score, no combo numeral, no "NICE." A pause control is an accessibility exception (see screen flow), visually quiet, and it does not score.

**Score after the beat, never on the best collision.** A CHAOS% popping while the copier eats the chair is a HUD novel. The punchline card is when numbers are allowed to exist, and only three of them, and they are jokes.

**Checkout special case.** Stage a portrait lane, not a wide store: scanner + bagging in the lower/mid third, soda pyramid and next-lane ASSISTANCE light in the upper third. If a cart must leave frame, the camera takes the player with it for one beat, then returns to the worst remaining thing.

**Failure of read = failure of design.** If QA cannot tell a stranger what caused the lamp without a replay scrubber, rewrite the chain, do not add arrows.

## Juice

Juice is how the chain is *sold*. Extra UI badges are how it is *explained*. Prefer selling.

**Camera.** Calm: locked, or a lazy vertical peek. Chain: follow the active object with soft lag. Peak: slow-mo (see below). Punchline: freeze on the stupidest still, then the card slides over it so the wreck stays visible in the gaps.

**Slow-mo.** Not always-on. Trigger on a *link*: the moment object A causes object B to become a problem. Two or three peaks per chain, not twelve. Audio stretches with the image. Then full speed so the rest can get worse.

**Haptics.** Three verbs, not a rumble bed.

1. Commit — a short, sharp tick. "You did that."
2. Impact / link — a heavier thud, scaled to mass. Printer thud ≠ paper flutter.
3. Punchline freeze — one longer hit, then silence in the hands.

If the device cannot haptic, the sound does the same grammar. Never require a badge that says COMBO x4.

**Sound.** Foley tells the chain. Office: cup slosh, printer grind, paper flock, lamp pop, copier scream. Checkout: scanner beep-loop, peas thud, soda avalanche, ASSISTANCE chime, coupon printer. Kitchen: toast, ceramic, a fridge magnet that becomes a projectile. Music is a low pulse in calm, ducks under the chain, stings the punchline. No lyrical hype drop. No corporate whoosh on every tap.

**Screen shake.** Peaks only. Constant shake makes the chain unreadable and makes people put the phone down.

**Haptics and sound > extra UI badges.** A floating "CHAOS +12" is a spreadsheet having a party. If a number must exist before the card, it should not.

## End card

The end card is a joke plus a verdict. It is not a career stats screen.

**Layout (portrait, wreck still in the background):**

```
[ frozen worst frame ]

THAT ESCALATED QUICKLY.

CHAOS                              97%
SATISFACTION                      112%
THINGS THAT DEFINITELY
WEREN'T YOUR FAULT                 43

[ AGAIN ]              [ CLIP ]
```

Three lines. Three numbers. One headline. Two thumb-sized actions. That is the whole product sentence.

**What the numbers mean, to a thumb:**

- **CHAOS** — how out of control it got. Not "objects broken." A tidy pile of broken mugs is damage. A copier walking into HR is chaos.
- **SATISFACTION** — juice and timing. Can go over 100%. That is the joke. It is allowed to be unfair in the player's favor.
- **THINGS THAT DEFINITELY WEREN'T YOUR FAULT** — count of escalations the physics (and the writers) piled on after the fuse. The mean statistic. Protect this label. Do not rename it "Bonus objectives."

Do not put Damage, Precision, Combos, Escalation on this card. Those can feed the three jokes under the hood. Showing six meters is a post-match RPG. The source conversation already found the voice. Keep it.

**Headline pool (rotate, situation-aware, dry):**

- THAT ESCALATED QUICKLY.
- THE PRINTER STARTED IT.
- NOT YOUR PROBLEM.
- THE SODA KNEW.
- YOU WERE HOLDING A RUBBER BAND.
- THE TOASTER KNEW.

Never: "Great job!" Never: "Victory." Never: XP, stars, or a chest.

**AGAIN** reloads the same situation, same constraint, calm, instantly. No interstitial. No ad. No "are you sure." The last fuse can leave a faint ghost for a second so retry is mastery, not amnesia — then the ghost dies. **CLIP** shares the chain, not the card. If share is not ready in the slice, CLIP can be present and honest: **CLIP (SOON)** is worse than hiding it. Prefer a working mute replay export, or omit CLIP until it exports the chain.

## Risks

1. **Tool tray gravity.** Every smash game will ask for a hammer dock. If it ships, Oopsplosion becomes Pocket Rage Room with extra copy. Reject loadouts in the slice.
2. **Unreadable physics.** Real destruction on a 6-inch portrait is noise. Authored links + camera + slow-mo are not optional juice. They are the joke engine.
3. **Checkout in portrait.** Wide lane, tiny scanner, unreadable fuse. Must be restaged as a vertical lane (bagging + soda pyramid) or the situation is unfinished. Do not add zoom. Do not bring traffic jam back.
4. **Constraint as busywork.** "Only 3 objects" plus a checklist HUD is a puzzle app. Constraints must be one spoken line and a physical limit, not a counter the player manages mid-chain.
5. **HUD during the chain.** Score-on-hit, combo popups, pause menus, banners. Each one is a hand on the shoulder during the punchline.
6. **First attempt dry.** If the obvious fuse does not chain, session one is a physics toy. Script a generous first office path. Hide the cleverness on retry.
7. **Retry friction.** Rewarded video, situation select, tool re-pick, long loads. Any of these and "one more try" dies.
8. **Spreadsheet scoring.** Six honest metrics on screen will be read as homework. Three joke stats or bust.
9. **Tutorial finger.** A pulsing hand on the cup for five seconds is the game admitting the fuse is not obvious. Make the cup obvious. Use a one-line constraint, not a coach overlay.
10. **Daily revenge in the MVP.** A 30-second daily is a different product (habit, notification, streak). It will steal the slice into meta. Out.
11. **Share-the-score.** Virality in the brief is the chain. If CLIP posts the end card, you have shared a spreadsheet with a funny font.
12. **Mean voice that becomes cruel.** Dry and a little mean is "THE PRINTER STARTED IT." Corporate-mean is "Time to crush your enemies, champ." Stay short. Do not insult the player's life. Insult the printer.

## UX MVP

Prove the fantasy with the fewest screens: **boot → calm office (title + constraint over the scene) → chain → punchline → again.**

**In:**

- One-handed portrait.
- Situation 1 playable end-to-end: **Sunday at the office**, constraint **one rubber band**, obvious cup fuse, authored chain that a stranger can retell.
- Punchline card with CHAOS, SATISFACTION, THINGS THAT DEFINITELY WEREN'T YOUR FAULT.
- Instant AGAIN on the same situation.
- Chrome hidden during the chain. Camera, 2–3 slow-mo links, haptic grammar, foley.
- Fat-finger fuse (cup / band stretch) that works without a tutorial overlay.
- Situation 2 and 3 as the same machine, not a new UX: **kitchen** (one object) and **checkout** (hands off the scanner), only after office has punched.

**Out:**

- Home, shop, currency, upgrades, daily revenge, streaks, leaderboards, tool inventory, six-stat postgame, pinch-zoom, mid-chain scoring, onboarding slideshow.
- CLIP if it cannot export the chain. Add it when the replay is the joke.

**Later (one line, not a screen):** Daily revenge — one new irritation a day — is a habit layer for after the loop is proven, not a reason the first session exists.

**Pass test:** a person holding a phone, silent, smiles when the copier goes, reads the card aloud, hits AGAIN before they talk. If they tap around during the chain looking for more buttons, the UI failed. If they ask what they were supposed to break, the fuse failed. If they say "cool rage room," the product failed.
